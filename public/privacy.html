Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Olea is a prototype / proof-of-concept. The app you can launch from this site runs entirely in your browser as a self-contained simulation. This policy explains what that means for your data.

What we collect

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up, login, wallet connection, or personal profile in the simulation.
  • No personal data. We do not ask for, store, or transmit your name, email, card details, or financial information.
  • No server storage. The simulation state (values, positions, activity) lives only in your browser tab and is discarded when you close it.

Cookies & analytics

The site sets no tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics. The only external request is to Google Fonts to load the typefaces used across the site.

Real integrations

If Olea is ever connected to a real, funded Robinhood agentic account, any handling of account data would occur through Robinhood’s own systems and be governed by Robinhood’s privacy terms, not this prototype.

Contact

Questions? Reach us on X at @oleacash.
