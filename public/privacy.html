Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Olea website and app (“Olea”, “we”, “us”) handle information. Olea is a prototype and proof-of-concept. The app you can launch from this site runs entirely in your browser as a self-contained simulation. We have deliberately built it to need as little information about you as possible.

The short version: No account. No wallet. No personal or financial data collected or stored on our servers. The simulation lives only in your browser tab.

1. Scope

This policy covers this website and the in-browser simulation. It does not cover Robinhood, its products, or any third-party site we link to, each of which has its own privacy practices. If Olea is ever connected to a real, funded Robinhood account, that activity would be governed by Robinhood’s privacy terms, not this prototype.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No accounts or sign-in. There is no registration, login, email capture, or user profile.
  • No financial information. We do not ask for or receive card numbers, bank details, balances, holdings, or trading history.
  • No wallet connection. The simulation does not connect a crypto wallet or request signatures.
  • No sale of data. We have no personal data to sell, share, or trade, and we never would.

3. Information handled locally

The simulation generates state as you use it: values, positions, an activity log, and your guardrail settings. This state is computed and held in your browser only. It is not transmitted to us and is discarded when you close or refresh the tab. We do not use cookies to track you.

4. Third-party services

  • Fonts. The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser requests these files from Google, which may log standard request metadata (such as IP address) as described in Google’s privacy policy.
  • Hosting. The site is served by a static host, which may keep standard, aggregated server logs (such as request times and IP addresses) for security and reliability. We do not use these to identify you.

5. Analytics & tracking

We do not run third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or cross-site trackers on this site.

6. Data security

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data for us to lose. The site is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and you use the site at your own discretion.

7. Children

Olea is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. International users

The site can be accessed globally. Because the simulation processes data only in your browser, no personal data crosses borders to us.

9. Your choices

You can clear the simulation state at any time by closing or refreshing the tab, and you can block Google Fonts or third-party requests using your browser settings if you prefer.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the project evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us on X at @oleacash.

Olea is an independent concept and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Robinhood Markets, Inc.
