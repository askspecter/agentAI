FAQ

Good questions.

Is this real money?+

The app you can launch here is a fully client-side simulation, so you can explore the mechanics safely. The architecture is built to connect to Robinhood’s Trading MCP and Robinhood Chain when wired to a real, funded agentic account.

How does Olea keep my money safe?+

Olea only ever operates inside a dedicated account you fund. Daily and per-trade caps, a manual-approval threshold, and a minimum card buffer bound every action, and everything settles on-chain for a transparent record.

What does it invest in?+

A tokenized-stock basket on Robinhood Chain (e.g. an “AI Megacap” mix), with idle reserve earning DeFi yield between buys. The agent rebalances toward your target weights automatically.

Do I have to change how I spend?+

No. You spend exactly like you do today. Olea works with the leftovers, the round-ups, so it never gets in the way of your daily life.

Which network is this on?+

Everything runs on the Robinhood network, Robinhood Chain (an Arbitrum Orbit L2) plus the Trading MCP, with no bridges or external venues.
