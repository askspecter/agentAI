How Olea works, end to end

What is Olea?

Olea is an autonomous agent on the Robinhood network that turns everyday card spending into a self-sustaining flywheel: it rounds up each purchase, invests the spare change in tokenized stocks on-chain, earns DeFi yield on the idle reserve, and uses that yield to auto-repay your card.

The loop

  • Spend. Use your Robinhood card as normal.
  • Round-up. The agent captures the spare change, up to 5×.
  • Invest. The change is deployed into a tokenized-stock basket on Robinhood Chain, always nudging toward your target weights.
  • Earn yield. A reserve slice sits in a DeFi pool earning yield between buys.
  • Auto-repay. Yield is swept back to pay down the card, closing the loop so your principal keeps working.

Guardrails

The agent runs inside a dedicated, funded account and can only ever touch what you allow:

  • Round-up multiplier and a minimum card buffer
  • Per-trade cap and daily invest cap
  • A manual-approval threshold for large deployments
  • On-chain settlement for a verifiable, real-time record

Architecture

The live app is a fully client-side simulation, so it runs on any static host with no backend. The simulation engine models a price feed, the tokenized-stock positions, the DeFi yield reserve, and the agent’s decisions.

For a real deployment, the codebase keeps clearly-marked integration seams:

  • Trading MCP: routes real orders through Robinhood’s Agentic Account.
  • Robinhood Chain: settlement of tokenized-stock buys and DeFi yield.
  • Card webhook: the inbound feed of card purchases that starts the loop.
